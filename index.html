Our Story

A Taste of Afghanistan in Brooklyn

At Khorasaan Kabab & Gyro, we bring generations of Afghan tradition to every plate. From sizzling kababs to perfectly seasoned rice platters, every dish is prepared with halal ingredients and a lot of heart.

Located on Bath Avenue in Brooklyn, we are proud to serve our community with generous portions, bold flavors, and the warm hospitality Afghanistan is known for.
